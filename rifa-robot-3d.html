One robot, both halves

Built on mBot2 running gear — CyberPi, the Shield, two 180 encoder motors and the quad RGB sensor — on a deck you cut yourself. No attachment swap: the collector and the shooter live on the same chassis at different heights — blades at 27 mm, shooter above 50 mm — and the wings fold back along the sides to clear the 30 cm box. Drag to rotate, pull the explode slider apart, and click any part for its dimensions and the rule that forces them.
